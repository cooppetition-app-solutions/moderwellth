## 2. Pillar: Health & Wellbeing
**LinkedIn**
Your body keeps score, and so does your mind.
We often treat wellbeing as something to "get to" once the important work is done. The reframe: **your health isn't a reward for productivity. It's the foundation of it.**
When your nervous system is calm and your energy is steady, every other pillar gets easier.
**What's one non-negotiable you protect for your wellbeing? Share it. Someone else might borrow it.**

**Facebook**

**Instagram**

## 4. Pillar: Growth
**LinkedIn**
Growth rarely feels like growth while it's happening. It usually feels like discomfort, uncertainty, or starting over.
The reframe: **"I'm not there yet" is not failure. It's evidence you're on the way.**
Getting stuck is one of the most human things there is. The question is whether you have the tools, the ideas and the support to get unstuck.
**What's something you're growing through right now?**

**Facebook**
Growth looks like: saying no, trying again, asking for help, learning something new 🌱 Which one are you practicing this month? Comment below!

**Instagram**
Swap these 🌱
❌ "I failed" → ✅ "I learned"
❌ "I have to" → ✅ "I get to"
❌ "Someday" → ✅ "Today, one step"
Which one do you need most? Comment 1, 2 or 3.

## 6. Pillar: Relationships
**LinkedIn**
The quality of our relationships shapes the quality of our lives: partners, kids, parents, friends and colleagues.
Yet we often invest more intention in our careers than in the people we come home to.
The reframe: **relationships aren't maintained by accident. They're tended on purpose.**
Sometimes that means an honest conversation. Sometimes it means getting support together.
